Wind and plume 风与火焰

Manchester 1985, simplified. 曼彻斯特事故的简化实验。

20 s average, 0.5 m grid. Fire settings were fixed against a still-air textbook plume before any wind ran. Aircraft and pool outline are approximate; the angle off the nose is my assumption. No burn-through, no cabin.
20 秒平均，50 厘米网格。火的参数在跑风之前已按静风教科书公式定好。飞机和油池形状是近似的，偏机头角度是我的假设。没有模拟烧穿，也没有客舱。
